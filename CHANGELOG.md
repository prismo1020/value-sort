# What changed

Plain notes on how this version differs from the original value sort.

**Fewer cards.** The deck went from 86 to 62. Cards that said the same thing
twice were combined, like Loving and Loved, Safety and Stability, Health and
Fitness, Authority and Power. Nothing was cut outright. Each one folded into
its twin.

**No ranking step.** You finish with your ten values instead of dragging them
into order. They are listed alphabetically so no order is implied.

**Your progress saves.** Close the tab and come back to it later. The old
version lost everything the moment you refreshed.

**You can undo.** Misclick on card 40 and you can take it straight back. The
old version gave you no way to fix a mistake.

**Falling short no longer restarts you.** If you ended the first round with
fewer than ten keepers, the old version showed a warning box and made you redo
every single card. Now it shows what you set aside and you promote a few.

**Faster to get through.** Arrow keys sort the cards, so you never have to
reach for the mouse.

**Works properly on a phone.** The old ranking screen needed drag and drop,
which barely worked on a touchscreen.

**You can keep your results.** Copy, download, or print them, along with a
chart of which themes your ten values cluster into.

**Less to read.** The intro blurb, the step-by-step instructions, and the
commentary on the results screen are gone. The exercise speaks for itself.

**Loads faster.** Roughly 600 KB of code became three small files, with
nothing to fetch from anywhere else.
